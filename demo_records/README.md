# Paperazzi public legacy-record demo set

These are genuine public historical scans. No document content was recreated or synthetically generated.

## 1. Philippines Military Memorandum Book and Map - Gazetteer page (1901)

File: `01_philippines_gazetteer_1901.jpg`

- Original: *Philippines military memorandum book and map*, Adolph von Brandis, 1901.
- Repository: Library of Congress; Internet Archive scan.
- Source record: https://www.loc.gov/item/01015044/
- Public scan: https://archive.org/details/philippinesmilit00bran
- Rights: public domain.
- Paperazzi value: aged paper, bleed-through, dense place-name records, populations, provinces, dates, coordinates, abbreviations, and multiple rows.
- Recommended demo: upload this image first. Show OCR regions, structured geographic entries, confidence/review routing, and source crops.

## 2. Map of the City of Manila and Vicinity (1919)

File: `02_manila_engineer_map_1919.jpg`

- Creator: Office of Department Engineer, Philippine Department; prepared June 1915 and corrected March 1919.
- Repository: Library of Congress Geography and Map Division.
- Source record: https://www.loc.gov/item/2012586259/
- Rights: Library of Congress Geography and Map Division digitized item; free to use and reuse unless a specific rights advisory states otherwise.
- Paperazzi value: blue-line technical map, faded labels, roads, blocks, waterways, contours, boundaries, scale, and dense geometry.
- Recommended demo: upload the full-resolution image. Focus on drawing-object overlays, map labels, source-linked OCR, and vector/CAD export. For camera scanning, print at A3 if possible; A4 makes labels very small.

## 3. Alexander Graham Bell telephone notebook, pages 40-41 (March 10, 1876)

File: `03_bell_telephone_notebook_1876.jpg`

- Creator: Alexander Graham Bell.
- Repository: Library of Congress Manuscript Division, Alexander Graham Bell Family Papers.
- Source record: https://www.loc.gov/item/magbell.25300201/
- Public image information: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AGBell_Notebook.jpg
- Rights: Bell's own manuscripts in the collection are public domain.
- Paperazzi value: cursive handwriting, corrections, line crossings, an early telephone schematic, labels, dates, and narrative notes.
- Recommended demo: print in landscape and use the iPhone camera scanner. Show perspective correction, handwriting OCR limitations, source evidence, the review queue, manual correction, and drawing-object detection.

## Suggested live sequence

1. Upload the 1901 gazetteer page for a fast, readable OCR and structuring result.
2. Camera-scan the Bell notebook in landscape to demonstrate difficult handwriting and human verification.
3. Upload the Manila map last for the strongest visual drawing/CAD-object result.

Do not promise perfect transcription of the Bell handwriting or tiny map labels. The strongest problem-solution story is that Paperazzi exposes uncertainty, links every result back to the scan, and makes correction and export practical.
